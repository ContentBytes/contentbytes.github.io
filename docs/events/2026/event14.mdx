# #14 | Let's talk AI

During this month meetup, we're asking a question that's hard to ignore right now: what happens to your content when AI is the one reading it?

## Topics and speakers

👉 **Tech writing that can take a question: a trainer’s notes on making docs work with AI**

The way customers consume technical documentation has shifted with the rise of AI tools. As chatbots, AI search engines and agents increasingly digest and serve our content, technical writers face a new challenge: structuring documentation to ensure AI systems generate accurate and complete outputs. Drawing from hands-on technical training experience, this talk connects the situations observed in live end-user training sessions to the ways AI now uses documentation. We will search for actionable strategies to help tech writers structure content for machines, while asking a question: how do we balance AI-digestibility with traditional, human-friendly readability? Moving beyond oversimplified 'AI acting as an user' analogies and basic clear-writing principles, this talk focuses on exploring the additional ways of structuring for the AI era.

by **Urszula Szuberska**

Urszula brings 9 years of technical writing experience, spanning both product and project documentation in Polish, Spanish, and English, alongside technical translation, and software localization. She also draws on her background as a technical trainer, where she was responsible for planning and delivering hands-on training programs for testers and users in the telecom industry.

👉 **​Feed the Machine: Patterns in How Agents Digest Content**

How do AI agents work under the hood? Learn how these independent actors browse the web, read files, and access data from vector stores. We'll also take a look at the latest trends and emerging patterns in the space of agentic development

by **Olga Stefaniuk**

Senior Developer Advocate at Box, former Frontend Developer, cognitive science graduate, WomenTech Network Leader of the Year Award 2025 Finalist. Now combining coding, community building, and knowledge sharing in Developer Relations. Interested in web accessibility, design, and artificial intelligence.
