# #16 | Let's talk AI reliability and docs quality

AI assistants can sound very sure of themselves. But where do their answers actually come from? 

## Topics and speakers

👉 **Architecting AI Reliability: The Role of High-Quality Documentation**

AI assistants are only as good as what they can retrieve. Anna will show how clear, complete docs keep RAG-based products on track, and what happens when engineering teams quietly fill the gaps with separate "AI-only" content. Spoiler: a workaround isn't a strategy. You'll leave with a case for bringing that knowledge back where both users and AI can find it.

by **Anna Bogusz**

Anna Bogusz is a technical writer with 14 years of experience making complex technology easier to understand. Her background spans technical documentation, UI content, translation, and leading technical writers on software projects. At Cisco Splunk, she plans and delivers documentation for a cloud platform and works with product, engineering, design, architecture, and support teams to improve user journeys. She also explores AI-assisted writing workflows, focusing on clear, consistent content and reliable information for AI agents. Anna is passionate about clarity, simplicity, and helping people find the information they need. Outside of work, she enjoys spending time with her family, hiking, reading, and gardening.
