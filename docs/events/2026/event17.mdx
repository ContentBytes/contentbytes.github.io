# #17 | Let's reboot minds and boundaries

December is coming, and with it the end-of-year rush. We are wrapping up projects, closing tickets, and saying “sure, I’ll take that too” one more time. Before the holidays, let’s hit pause together.

​Join us for our last meetup of 2026, an evening about slowing down, resetting, and walking into the new year a little lighter.

## Topics and speakers

👉 **Debugging your mind: mental health in tech**

by **Klaudia Grzondziel**

Klaudia is a Technical Writer with 8+ years of experience. She is passionate about information architecture, docs-as-code, and making complicated information easier to follow. In her free time, she enjoys exploring her creativity. She also writes articles to dev.to, covering topics like technical documentation, UX, and mental health in tech.

👉 **Hat Check: the art of saying no** 

by **Katarzyna Zielińska**

​Katarzyna is a Project Manager on Cisco’s Global Benefits team. She brings 8+ years of experience from Cisco and Shell, helping global teams make change actually stick, from redesigning ways of working to coaching people leaders. She’s also a Wellbeing Ambassador at Cisco, part of the Pavelka programme and a long distance swimmer. With that many hats, she’s learned that the real skill isn’t juggling them. It’s knowing when to put one down.
